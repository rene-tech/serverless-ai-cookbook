Online documentation can describe a newer version than the selected NGC binary.
The live App's pinned image, version, build features and qualification evidence
determine what is supported. NVIDIA's charts may use another NAMD version,
multiple GPUs and concurrent simulations; do not compare those numbers directly
with one GPU/one trajectory without matching the experimental conditions.
