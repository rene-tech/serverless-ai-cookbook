Methods and standards to select according to study type:
- NIH rigor/reproducibility: https://grants.nih.gov/policy-and-compliance/policy-topics/reproducibility/guidance
- PRISMA 2020: https://www.prisma-statement.org/prisma-2020
- ARRIVE 2.0: https://arriveguidelines.org/arrive-guidelines
- FAIR principles: https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/
- Clinical trial records: https://clinicaltrials.gov/
- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ and Europe PMC: https://europepmc.org/
- Squair et al., donor-aware differential expression: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2

Use reporting frameworks to identify missing information, not to certify an
incomplete study. Literature searches, narrative evidence maps and systematic
reviews have different coverage and reproducibility requirements.
