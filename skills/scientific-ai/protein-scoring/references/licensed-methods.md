# FoldX and CamSol

These methods require the actual licensed implementation and hosting entitlement.
An installed skill is not evidence that either App is available. Check the live
authorised catalog and model schema. If absent, explain that specific limitation;
do not substitute a home-made formula or claim a local package is licensed.

For FoldX, first distinguish folding stability from protein–protein binding
effects. Use the exposed operation appropriate to the question, its original
reference structure and exact mutation numbering. Keep RepairPDB/preparation
explicit and retain both original and prepared structures. Compare matched
wild-type/mutant runs and report the implementation's ΔΔG sign, kcal/mol units,
settings and replicate variability. Do not mix unrelated structures or score
functions as if they were a controlled comparison.

For CamSol, distinguish sequence-only intrinsic solubility from any supported
structure-corrected mode. Use the licensed version's exact input conditions and
score definition. A solubility score is not binding affinity, aggregation kinetics
or an absolute concentration unless the supplied method explicitly calibrates
that quantity. Keep score direction, sequence and preprocessing in the report.

Primary sources: [FoldX licensing and services](https://foldxsuite.crg.es/licensing-and-services),
[CamSol method](https://licensing.enterprise.cam.ac.uk/product/camsol-method).
